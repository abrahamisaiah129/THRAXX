Company Boilerplate

Traxx (traxx.ng) is a Nigerian fleet intelligence and live telemetry platform that eliminates operational blindspots, unverified driver detours, and fuel leakage for commercial dispatch operators across Nigeria. Founded in Lagos, Traxx combines real-time GPS tracking, automated anomaly flags, and shift-based privacy controls engineered specifically for African road and network conditions.

Headquarters: Lagos, Nigeria
Coverage: Nationwide across all 36 states and the FCT
Registration: CAC Registered & NDPR Compliant
