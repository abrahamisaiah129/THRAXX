Platform capabilities

Built for the way Nigerian fleets actually work.

We do not just show you where your vehicles are. We help you stay in control of what is happening.

Live Fleet Map

Every vehicle on your payroll, live on one map. GPS positions refresh every 5 seconds with colour coded status: on trip, idle, flagged, or offline.

Automated Flag Detection

Route deviations, unassigned movement, idle alerts, and unauthorised disengagement are all flagged the moment they happen. Not the next day.

GPS Replay and Audit Trail

Replay any rider's full day of GPS movement, frame by frame. Export as PDF or CSV. An irrefutable record for every disciplinary decision.

Shift Management and Sign Off

Riders clock in through the app. Every sign off requires your explicit approval. No automatic approvals, ever. Every shift is logged for payroll verification.

Rider Scorecards

On time rates, total flags, and trips completed for every rider, updated daily. Built to support performance reviews with numbers, not opinions.

Fair Flagging Engine

Heavy traffic is not a flag. Traxx tells the difference between a rider stuck in genuine congestion and one who has left their route on purpose. Riders on the correct route are never penalised.
