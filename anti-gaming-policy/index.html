Anti-Gaming & Tampering Policy

Zero tolerance for mock locations, coordinate spoofing, SIM tampering, and fraudulent delivery check-ins across commercial fleets.

1.0 Purpose & Policy Scope

To preserve the absolute commercial integrity of delivery logistics across Nigeria, Traxx maintains an aggressive, automated Anti-Gaming protocol. This policy defines prohibited fraudulent actions and outlines technical enforcement mechanisms applied to drivers, contractors, and fleet operators.

2.0 Prohibited Tampering Behaviors

The following activities are strictly prohibited and immediately flagged as malicious tampering:

  • Mock Location Emulation: Utilizing mock location software, virtual GPS emulators, or Android developer hooks to report fabricated coordinates.
  • Hardware Disconnection: Deliberately severing power leads, wrapping GPS antennas in foil/insulation materials (Faraday shielding), or removing device SIM cards.
  • Shift Gaming: Clocking into shifts while stationary or delegating smartphone devices to unauthorized third parties to falsify working hours.
  • Phantom Delivery Stamps: Marking drop-off destinations as completed when coordinates indicate the vehicle was outside the required delivery perimeter.

3.0 Automated Detection Systems

Traxx algorithms analyze multiple telemetry vectors simultaneously: speed continuity, cellular tower triangulation (LBS verification), accelerometer variance, and satellite constellation count. When an anomaly is detected, the event is immediately flagged with high-confidence forensic badges.

4.0 Consequences of Verified Gaming

Verified tampering incidents generate an unalterable incident report forwarded to the fleet operator's executive management. Penalties may include immediate driver suspension from the platform, termination of operator agreements, and referral of serial fraud to relevant authorities.
